# I Choose Me 💖

**👉 [annegaba.github.io/ichooseme](https://annegaba.github.io/ichooseme/)**

About to text your ex? Take this little check-in first, girlie.

A free page for the girlies to visit whenever the urge to message him hits. No sign-ups, no subscriptions, just a quick reset back to choosing yourself.

## How it works

- **5 questions, one at a time.** The first and last ask the big one: *"Are you getting back together with him?"* Good luck clicking **Yes**. It moves, vanishes, or locks itself. And if you somehow catch it anyway: *"You think you sneaky huh? You still ain't getting back together with him."* 😌
- **Lock In, Girliepop!** Answer "no" to a self-check (your promise to yourself, self-care, water and a real meal) and you get a focus page with a little checklist made just for that.
- **Make it through** and you'll be told what you already know: *"You got this girlie! Say it out loud: I choose me!"*
- **Pomodoro timer 🍅** built in, for when it's time to really lock in. Focus and break modes, custom minutes, and a cute chime when time's up.
- **Printable lock-in sheet 🖨️** with your checklist, pomodoro tomatoes to color in, and space to write what you'll do instead of texting him.
